Research provenance
====================

This independent research implementation is not sponsored or endorsed by Nikon.
Nikon model names identify the interoperability target. The original resource744
network body and model-specific tables are distinguished from the authored CPU
implementation; no blanket software license is asserted for those model assets.
The optional host decoder is not a vendor SDK or simulator binary.

qsort-reference.c retains its original BSD notice. Public Python dependency
wheels retain their original distribution metadata and license files. No private
chip documentation, manuals, courses, toolchains or firmware image is included.
