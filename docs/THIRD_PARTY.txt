Third-party components
======================

The public PyTorch, NumPy, Pillow and transitive dependency wheels retain their
original distribution metadata and license files. Exact versions and hashes are
listed in requirements-lock.txt and docs/wheelhouse-index.json.
qsort-reference.c retains its original BSD notice.
The included host decoder is a research reference, not a vendor toolchain binary.
The original model body and selected captured tensors are network reproduction
artifacts and are identified separately from the Python software dependencies.
No private chip documentation, manuals, courses, SDKs or tools are distributed.
