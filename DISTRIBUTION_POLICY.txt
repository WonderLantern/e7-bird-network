Reviewed public distribution
============================

Included: original network-specific model body and decoded command metadata,
authored numerical implementation, optional host reference decoder and source,
two procedural physical fixtures, English documentation, redacted numerical
reports and portable experiments. Offline runtime assets also include pinned
public Python wheels with their original notices and metadata.

Excluded: photographs and screenshots (including their planar tensor forms),
photographic network heads/activations, all raw device parameter/record/session
buffers, unselected workspace, local paths, machine/account identifiers,
credentials and keys. Private chip manuals, courses, SDKs, toolkits, vendor
compiler/simulator binaries, firmware images and firmware code dumps are absent.

The public Git history is created independently from the private research tree.
Public dependencies are provided as release assets, not committed to source Git.
No previous complete-history or unredacted English archive is nested or uploaded.
